How to Play Minesweeper

Rules:

  • The goal is to uncover all cells that don't contain mines.
  • Numbers show how many mines are adjacent to that cell.
  • Use logic to determine which cells are safe to click.

Controls:

  • Left click: Reveal a cell
  • Right click: Flag a suspected mine
  • Game face: Click to start a new game

Difficulty Levels:

  • Beginner: 9x9 grid with 10 mines
  • Intermediate: 16x16 grid with 40 mines
  • Expert: 16x30 grid with 99 mines
